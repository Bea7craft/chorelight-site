ChoreLight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Your family data stays with your family. ChoreLight does not operate its own servers that collect or read your household board. Chores, allowances, and profiles live on your devices and in your Apple iCloud account so your family can sync. Liam Mill cannot browse or download your family’s private CloudKit data. If you choose to turn on crash reports (off unless you switch it on), the App sends technical crash reports to a service we use, described below. They never include your family’s names, chores, allowances, or photos.

ChoreLight (“the App”) is a family chore, allowance, and calendar helper provided by Liam Mill, a sole trader in Australia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

Who this app is for

ChoreLight is intended for families. Children may use the App under parent supervision. Parents should review settings and who has access to the shared household iCloud account.

What we do not do

  • We do not host a ChoreLight backend that stores your chore board for us to inspect.
  • We do not sell your family data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers in the App.
  • We do not require a separate “ChoreLight account” or email signup.

Data the App stores

Depending on how you use the App, it may store:

  • Family member names and roles (parent / kid)
  • Chore titles, schedules, completion status, and related notes
  • Allowance amounts, deductions, and money added back
  • Optional photos a child attaches when marking a chore done (up to two per chore, from the camera or the photo library)
  • Optional PINs used only inside the App for profile / parent tools
  • Manual family calendar events you create in the App
  • Feature ideas you submit inside the App. Submitting an idea can also open an email to ChoreLight on that phone

Where data is stored (private to your household)

  • On your device — a local cache so the App works offline.
  • In your iCloud account (CloudKit) — so devices can share one household board.

By default, sync uses your private CloudKit database for devices signed into the same Apple ID.

Optionally, a parent can invite other Apple IDs (for example a partner or older child with their own iCloud account) using Apple’s CloudKit sharing. Invitees who accept can access the same household board according to the permission granted in the invite. Only invite people you trust.

That is your family’s space, not a public feed and not a developer dashboard of users. Apple’s privacy policy also applies to iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Photos

When a child marks a chore done, they can attach up to two photos using the camera or the photo library. Those photos are stored with the household board on the device and in the family’s iCloud, so a parent can see them. ChoreLight does not upload them to a server we operate, and Liam Mill cannot view them. Appearance choices (light, dark, and colour) stay on that phone only and are not part of the shared board.

Calendars

If you allow Calendar access, the App reads events from the device Calendar app (for example iCloud, Google, or other calendars on the phone) to show them on Today and Calendar screens. Calendar content is not uploaded to a ChoreLight server; display stays on device via Apple’s EventKit APIs.

Face ID / biometrics

On parent phones that choose “stay signed in,” Face ID (or Touch ID) is used only on that device to unlock the App. Biometric data does not leave the device; authentication is handled by iOS.

Crash reports (optional, off by default)

From version 1.2, ChoreLight has a Send crash reports setting. It is off when you install the App. No crash reports are sent unless a parent turns it on. The setting is only in the parent area of the App, and turning it on needs the parent PIN. It can be turned off at any time. The App then stops collecting reports and deletes any reports still waiting on the device to be sent.

When the setting is on and the App crashes or freezes, the App sends a technical report so we can find and fix the bug. We use Sentry, a service provided by Functional Software, Inc. (doing business as Sentry), which processes these reports for us as a service provider.

A report includes:

  • Crash details: the error type and the stack trace (where in the App’s code it failed)
  • Device and app details: device model, iOS version, free memory and storage, App version and build
  • A short list of technical steps before the crash (for example which screen was open), with any text removed

Before a report leaves your device, the App removes user and installation identifiers, IP addresses, and free text. Reports do not include family member names, chore titles, allowance amounts, photos, PINs, calendar events, or iCloud data. We do not send screenshots or screen recordings, usage or performance tracking, or app-session data. We do not use reports for advertising, tracking, or profiling. Sentry is set so that IP addresses are not stored, and its server-side data scrubbing removes common sensitive values (such as passwords, card numbers and IP addresses) if any reach it.

Where and how long: Sentry stores crash reports in the United States. This means the information in a report is disclosed to an overseas recipient. Before that disclosure we took reasonable steps, as required by Australian Privacy Principle 8: we turn reports off unless a parent opts in, remove identifiers and free text on the device before sending, have a data processing agreement with Sentry, and have set Sentry to not store IP addresses and to apply its server-side data scrubbing to every report. Sentry deletes crash reports automatically after 30 days. Sentry’s privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy.

Children: ChoreLight is used by children with a parent’s supervision. Crash reports are opt-in, controlled by the parent, contain no content that children add, and are never used to identify or contact anyone. We collect them only to keep the App working, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (including APP 8 on overseas disclosure), the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (support for internal operations), and the EU/UK GDPR (consent; data minimisation).

Data we do not collect

Liam Mill does not collect your family’s chore data or photos. Family member names, chores, allowances, PINs, calendar events, and chore photos stay on your devices and in your family’s own iCloud account. We cannot access them, and they are never sent to us or to Sentry.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers in the App.
  • We do not require a separate ChoreLight account or email signup.

Sharing

Household data is available to:

  • Devices using the same Apple ID for iCloud, and/or
  • People who were explicitly invited via ChoreLight’s “Invite family” share and accepted with their Apple ID.

Apple Family Sharing membership alone does not grant access. Choose a strong Apple ID password and two-factor authentication. Only send invites to trusted family members.

Children

Parents control family members, chores, and allowances. Use shared-device profile mode for kids when appropriate. Do not store sensitive information beyond normal chore and allowance use.

Your choices

  • Sign out of iCloud or remove the App to stop new sync from that device.
  • Delete household data from within the App (where available) or by clearing iCloud data for the App via Apple’s tools / reinstalling and starting empty after removing cloud records.
  • Revoke Calendar, Camera, or Face ID access in iOS Settings → ChoreLight.
  • Skip photos when marking a chore done. Photos are optional.
  • Leave Settings → Send crash reports off (the default), or turn it off at any time.
