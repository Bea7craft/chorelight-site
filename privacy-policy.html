ChoreLight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Your family data stays with your family. ChoreLight does not operate its own servers that collect or read your household board. Chores, allowances, and profiles live on your devices and in your Apple iCloud account so your family can sync. The developer cannot browse or download your family’s private CloudKit data.

ChoreLight (“the App”) is a family chore, allowance, and calendar helper. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

Who this app is for

ChoreLight is intended for families. Children may use the App under parent supervision. Parents should review settings and who has access to the shared household iCloud account.

What we do not do

  • We do not host a ChoreLight backend that stores your chore board for us to inspect.
  • We do not sell your family data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers in the App.
  • We do not require a separate “ChoreLight account” or email signup.

Data the App stores

Depending on how you use the App, it may store:

  • Family member names and roles (parent / kid)
  • Chore titles, schedules, completion status, and related notes
  • Allowance amounts and deductions
  • Optional PINs used only inside the App for profile / parent tools
  • Manual family calendar events you create in the App
  • Feature ideas you submit inside the App

Where data is stored (private to your household)

  • On your device — a local cache so the App works offline.
  • In your iCloud account (CloudKit) — so devices can share one household board.

By default, sync uses your private CloudKit database for devices signed into the same Apple ID.

Optionally, a parent can invite other Apple IDs (for example a partner or older child with their own iCloud account) using Apple’s CloudKit sharing. Invitees who accept can access the same household board according to the permission granted in the invite. Only invite people you trust.

That is your family’s space, not a public feed and not a developer dashboard of users. Apple’s privacy policy also applies to iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Calendars

If you allow Calendar access, the App reads events from the device Calendar app (for example iCloud, Google, or other calendars on the phone) to show them on Today and Calendar screens. Calendar content is not uploaded to a ChoreLight server; display stays on device via Apple’s EventKit APIs.

Face ID / biometrics

On parent phones that choose “stay signed in,” Face ID (or Touch ID) is used only on that device to unlock the App. Biometric data does not leave the device; authentication is handled by iOS.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers in the App.
  • We do not require a separate ChoreLight account or email signup.

Sharing

Household data is available to:

  • Devices using the same Apple ID for iCloud, and/or
  • People who were explicitly invited via ChoreLight’s “Invite family” share and accepted with their Apple ID.

Apple Family Sharing membership alone does not grant access. Choose a strong Apple ID password and two-factor authentication. Only send invites to trusted family members.

Children

Parents control family members, chores, and allowances. Use shared-device profile mode for kids when appropriate. Do not store sensitive information beyond normal chore and allowance use.

Your choices

  • Sign out of iCloud or remove the App to stop new sync from that device.
  • Delete household data from within the App (where available) or by clearing iCloud data for the App via Apple’s tools / reinstalling and starting empty after removing cloud records.
  • Revoke Calendar or Face ID access in iOS Settings → ChoreLight.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: chorelightsupport@icloud.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
