ChoreLight Support

Need help with ChoreLight (family chores, allowances, and iCloud sync)?

Email: chorelightsupport@icloud.com

We aim to reply as soon as we can. Please include your device type (iPhone/iPad) and whether you use TestFlight or the App Store build.

Marking a chore done

A child signed in on Today taps I’m done. They can add up to two photos from the Camera or the Photo library, or send it with no photo. A parent then approves it on Today or under Parent → Approvals. The photos stay in the family’s iCloud board.

Money added back

On a parent phone, each child’s card has Add money back for an extra amount and a reason. The same control is under Parent → Allowances.

Look of the app

Settings → Appearance chooses System, Light, or Dark, and a colour. That choice stays on this phone.

Refresh and switching person

Pull down on Today to refresh. The button at the top left shows who is signed in. Tap Switch to change profile.

Ideas

Parent → Ideas saves a request on the household board and opens an email to chorelightsupport@icloud.com. Send the email if you want ChoreLight to read it. The list is only visible to your family.
